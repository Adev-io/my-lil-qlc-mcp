# Operating Playbook — Punk Rig

How to run the whole show from your head, without getting lost in the options.

Everything here is by **name + pad position (R#C#)** — never colour. R1C1 = top-left pad,
R8C8 = bottom-right. The Launchpad, the on-screen console, and the dashboard all use the same grid.

---

## 1. The whole rig in one picture

Three groups of lights. **Each owns its own beams — they never fight each other.**

- **PARS** (10 floor cans) + **BARS** (2 up high) = your **WASH** — the colour field.
- **HEADS** (2 movers) = your **beams** — where they point, what colour, how they move.

Every moment on stage is just **four questions**:

1. What **colour** is the wash?
2. Where do the **heads** point + what colour?
3. Is anything **moving**?
4. Any one-shot **hit**?

## 2. Two rules govern everything

- **One-per-row (solo):** press a second button in the same row and the first drops. You *can't*
  pile up clashing looks inside a row — the console protects you.
- **Last-press-wins:** if two buttons touch the same light, the newest press wins. Movement only
  touches pan/tilt, so it rides **on top** of whatever colour is already up.

## 3. The shortcut that removes the overwhelm

**The 15 PRESETS are complete looks.** One tap sets pars + bars + head colour + head aim + gobo,
all at once. **You can run an entire show from the PRESETS alone.**

So the real workflow per moment is:

1. **Tap a PRESET** for the song moment → done, full look.
2. *Optional:* add **one** MOVE for energy.
3. *Optional:* swap **one** WASH colour for variety.
4. *Optional:* **one** Strobe hit on the big accent.

That's it. Everything else (per-group colour, gobos, FX, faders) lives on the two advanced pages
and is *seasoning* — reach for it only when you want it.

## 5. The four "out-of-character" traps

1. **Movement with no head colour → heads go dark.** A bare MOVE only drives pan/tilt; if nothing
   set the head colour, there's nothing to light. **Fix:** start from a PRESET (it sets head colour),
   *then* add the MOVE.
2. **"Auto" looks only animate on the real rig.** `Pars Auto` / `Bars Auto` (advanced page) hand off
   to the fixture's firmware — they look **dead in the monitor**. Use the **`Morph`** twins to see and
   control the motion.
3. **Blackout latches.** `R8C7` kills output and *stays* killed — tap it **again** to clear before the
   next look, or the stage stays dark. Never end a song on Blackout and walk away.
4. **Don't over-layer.** Trust the PRESET. Add at most one MOVE + one WASH. More than that and looks
   start fighting for attention.

## 6. Plan a song in your head

A song is just a **spine of PRESETS** with the hits marked. For each section pick one PRESET; add a
MOVE only where you want more energy. Write it by **pad position**, so under stage lights you're
hitting a *spot*, not remembering a colour.

### Worked example — a punk song

| Section | Tap | Look | + MOVE (optional) | Note |
|---------|-----|------|-------------------|------|
| Intro | R1C1 | Opener | — | bring it up clean |
| Verse 1 | R1C2 | Verse Blue | R6C4 Head Sweep Slow | calm, cool |
| Chorus 1 | R1C3 | Chorus Warm | R6C2 Audience Sweep | lift energy, throw to crowd |
| Verse 2 | R2C1 | Deep Verse | — | settle back down |
| Chorus 2 | R1C8 | Magenta Pop | R6C2 Audience Sweep | punchier than chorus 1 |
| Breakdown | R1C4 | Breakdown UV | R6C6 Figure 8 | dark, moody, hypnotic |
| Build | R1C5 | Anthem | tap R8C1 Strobe on the hits | everything up |
| Finish | R2C7 | Punk Slam | R6C3 Cross Zigzag | the slam |
| End | R8C7 | Blackout | — | tap once to kill — **tap again to clear** for the next song |

Everything else is optional.
